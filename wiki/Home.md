# Lightman's Currency: Claim Economy

Welcome to the wiki for **Claim Economy** — a NeoForge mod that connects [Lightman's Currency](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lightmans-currency) to your claim mod of choice, turning land claiming and protection into a real, ongoing economy.

> **Minecraft:** 1.21.1 · **Loader:** NeoForge · **Side:** Both (required on server and client) · **Current version:** 4.4.0

## Two backends, your choice

The mod supports either of two claim backends:

- **FTB Chunks + FTB Teams + FTB Library** — the full-featured integration: land/build chunk split, per-chunk player permissions, wars, siege mode, bounties, a player marketplace, the Pioneer Bonus, and native UI built into the FTB claim/team screens.
- **Open Parties and Claims (OP&C)** — a lighter integration covering claiming, upkeep, and force-load billing via a command instead of custom UI.

If both are installed, the FTB integration is used. See [Features](Features) for exactly what each backend supports.
